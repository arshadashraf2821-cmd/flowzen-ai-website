Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions to ask before you start.

Every solution is scoped around your systems, requirements, and the level of automation that makes sense.

Do you build websites, or only automation?

Both. We design and build business websites, landing pages, service websites, and real estate property showcase websites. A website can also be connected to WhatsApp, email, and enquiry workflows where suitable. Features that need a database, user accounts, or secure server-side processing are scoped separately.

What types of businesses can Flowzen AI help?

We focus on practical solutions for businesses such as real estate agencies, salons, restaurants, clinics, retail and e-commerce teams, hospitality businesses, and professional service firms. The best starting point is a clear business need, such as a website or a repetitive task.

Do I need to replace my existing business software?

Not necessarily. We first review the tools you already use and whether they provide suitable integrations or APIs. The available options depend on each platform and your current setup.

Can you automate WhatsApp messages and follow-ups?

Where supported, we can design WhatsApp Business workflows for enquiries, approved templates, reminders, and follow-ups. Setup must follow the relevant platform rules, messaging permissions, and customer consent requirements.

Can an AI assistant make bookings automatically?

A workflow can support booking requests and confirmations when it is connected to a suitable calendar or reservation system with reliable availability data. Availability checks and booking actions must be configured and tested before use.

Will AI handle every customer conversation?

No. A good design defines what the assistant can answer and when it must hand a conversation to a person. Sensitive, unclear, or exceptional cases should be routed to authorised staff.

How do we get started?

Contact us by email or WhatsApp and tell us what you need: a website, or one task that takes too much time. We can discuss your goals, what information is available, and whether our services are a suitable fit.
